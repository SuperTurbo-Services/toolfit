# Commercial use

This skill is published under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0**
(`LICENSE`, SPDX id `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`). That licence is deliberately
generous in one direction and closed in the other.

## Free, no permission needed, no payment

- Using it yourself, on your own recordings, as a private individual
- Study, experiment, hobby projects, teaching yourself
- Modifying it for your own use
- Sharing your modified copy, as long as this licence and the
  `Required Notice:` line travel with it
- Use by a charity, school, university, public research body, public safety or
  health organisation, environmental organisation, or a government institution

## Needs a commercial licence

Anything commercial. Most commonly:

- Using it to produce subtitles for a paying client
- Running it inside a business as part of how that business makes money
- Bundling it into a product or service you sell
- Offering it as a hosted or managed service

If you are unsure which side of the line you are on, assume you need one and ask.
Asking is free and the answer is usually yes.

## Getting one

Commercial licences are available from SuperTurbo. Reach out through the channel
you were given this skill on, describe roughly what you want to do with it, and
you will get terms back.

## Why not a permissive licence

The engineering in this skill, specifically the silence anchored windowing that
keeps the second half of a recording in sync, is the product. Publishing it
permissively would mean giving away the thing being sold. Publishing it under
noncommercial terms means an individual can pick it up, run it on their own
video tonight, and pay nothing, which is the outcome worth having.

This file is a plain language summary. The `LICENSE` file is the agreement, and
it governs where the two differ.
